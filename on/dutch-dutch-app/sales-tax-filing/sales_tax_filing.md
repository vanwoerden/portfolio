# Sales tax filing

## Ideal state 2026
date: April 2023
We imagined what a perfect indirect tax world would look like 3 years out. The input was 40-some interviews with SMBs and accountants. This was just a few months after ChatGPT had launched, and while text wasn't yet a strong suit of LLMs, Midjourney proved to be extremely useful (and equally frustrating) for helping us visualize 4 key future scenarios. 

## Historical context
Historically, sales tax had always been a compliance thing; it was never seen as a revenue opportunity. We had acquired a business that specialized in US sales tax filing, but integrating that into QuickBooks and making it work as a business hadn't been successful the first time around (circa 2017). 

5 years later, the market for sales tax filing seemed a lot more attractive, and the team got executive buy in to build towards a profitable sales tax filing business. 

## Foundational research into filing
date: May 2024
I was put on as the lead designer. My first move was to try and understand the filing journey in detail, for both SMBs and accountants. One trick I employed was sending the interview participants a canvas before the session, with the ask to map their filing process in terms of actions, roles, highs, and lows. This is a tactic our of the context-mapping playbook called sensitization; essentially by having participants create something, and think about the topic in advance, you can prime them and they have something to reflect on immediately when the session starts. This way the output of our sessions had a lot more detail to it. I got about a 2/3 response to this request, with the remaining 1/3 we just mapped during the interview.
